Backyard Camping Guide

Everyone loves a good backyard campout once in a while! See more camping tips in our full guide.

What You'll Need

  • Tent
  • Sleeping bags
  • Flashlight
  • Marshmallows
  • Bug spray

Setting Up Your Campsite

  1. Pick a flat, dry spot in the yard.
  2. Lay down a tarp before pitching the tent.
  3. Set up the tent and stake it down.
  4. Arrange sleeping bags and pillows inside.
  5. Set up a safe campfire area away from the tent.

Things Every Camper Loves:

  • s'mores
  • stargazing
  • campfire stories

Top 3 Camping Mistakes:

  1. Forgetting bug spray
  2. Sleeping without a trap
  3. Ignoring the weather forecast
